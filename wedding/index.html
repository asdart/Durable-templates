Our Love Story

"The best thing to hold onto in life is each other."

We met on a rainy Tuesday in October 2017 at a small bookshop on the corner of Maple Street. Karl was reaching for the same dog-eared copy of "Norwegian Wood" that Emma had spotted from across the room. That shared love of Murakami turned into shared coffee, which turned into shared everything.

Eight years, three apartments, one rescue dog named Miso, and countless adventures later — we decided it was time to make it official. We can't wait to celebrate with the people who've made our story so beautiful.
