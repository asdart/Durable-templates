COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?

We welcome walk-ins every day, but we highly recommend booking an appointment to guarantee your preferred artist and time slot. You can book online or call us directly.

How long does a typical tattoo session take?

Session length varies by design complexity. Small pieces take 1–2 hours, while larger work like sleeves can be 6–8 hour sessions spread across multiple appointments. We’ll discuss timeline during your consultation.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. A non-refundable deposit is required at booking to secure your appointment.

Can I request a specific artist?

Absolutely. Each of our artists has their own specialty and style. We encourage you to browse their portfolios and request whoever resonates with your vision. Book early for preferred availability.

Do you offer services for minors?

Oregon law requires all tattoo clients to be 18 or older with valid photo ID. We do not make exceptions. We’re happy to schedule a consultation for your 18th birthday though!

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows will forfeit their deposit. We understand life happens — please communicate with us and we’ll do our best to accommodate reschedules.
